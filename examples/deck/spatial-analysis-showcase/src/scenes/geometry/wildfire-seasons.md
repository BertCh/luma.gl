## These are final perimeter records {#records}

Every loaded polygon has an archive **perimeter record date**. The live cartouche and readouts report the current qualifying record count, agency acreage, date span and polygon-part count. Ring area is proportional to acres; colour means record time only. These are final perimeter records in a subset, not an ignition list or a fire-growth animation.

## Record dates arrive through the seasons {#play}

The docked timeline, map clock and symbols share one playhead. Press **Play** to move through perimeter record dates; reduced motion opens paused. The cumulative agency-acre line is calculated from the loaded rows, while the newest record receives an achromatic halo so size never has to mean age.

## A fading window is GPU state {#window}

`GPUTimeWindowFilter` receives the current date, the **Trail length** and the old-end fade. It writes accepted rows and fade weights without rebuilding a CPU list. The timeline bracket, map symbols and clock are all bound to those same options; the live readouts distinguish GPU count, shown records and the newest accepted row.

## GPU acres reproduce source polygons {#ledger}

The ledger compares WGS84 GPU area with the agency acreage carried by each loaded record. Ghost bars provide the grouped comparison and the scatter plots GPU minus agency acres, with visible N and live median and maximum relative differences. It is a measurement ledger, not another projection lesson.

## A few records dominate the subset {#tour}

The tour resolves its ranked record from current loaded rows. Its name, acreage, date, share and cumulative contribution are live rather than frozen prose. The limits remain important: this archive is a subset of final perimeter records, and record dates do not describe ignition, spread or containment timing.
