Each tab runs one family of [spatial analysis contributors](/docs/api-reference/experimental/geospatial) on open
datasets from deck.gl-data. A mode compiles its `GPUCommandGraph` once, rewrites small parameter
buffers every frame (viewport, time window, radius, source node, observer, screen-space error), and
encodes into deck.gl's own frame encoder. deck.gl layers draw the outputs directly from GPU storage
buffers and indirect draw records; only small summaries are read back. The footer's rebuild counter
stays at zero while you drag sliders and the map.
