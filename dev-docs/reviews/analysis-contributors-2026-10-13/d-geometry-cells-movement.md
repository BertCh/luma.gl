# Stream D: correctness of the non-statistics half of `gpu-spatial-analysis/`

Repo `~/Documents/GitHub/vis.gl-build/luma-contributors`. Paths are relative to
`modules/experimental/src/gpu-spatial-analysis/` unless stated otherwise. Sub-reviews are in
`work/D/D1.md` (predicates, join, overlay, topology, buffer), `work/D/D2.md` (line and polygon
processing, tessellation, measures) and `work/D/D3.md` (cells/DGGS, movement). Scripts are in
`work/D/d1|d2|d3/`. The oracle venv is `work/D/geo-venv`: shapely 2.2 with GEOS 3.14, h3 4.5,
s2sphere and pyproj.

No GPU specs were run. Every row below was re-read in code by the coordinator. Where a row says
"script", a CPU re-implementation of the kernel arithmetic was compared with shapely, scipy or
h3, or with exact rationals.

## 1. Verdict

The base layer is sound:
- The exact orientation predicate (`segment-intersection/exact-orientation-wgsl.ts`) is correct, and so is the exact incircle.
- The H3, S2, geohash and quadkey forward code and the H3 neighbours and pentagons route around the shared gpgpu H3 bugs, and are checked against h3-js in node specs.
- Time handling is consistent across the trajectory modules and track similarity (relative f32, double-single or exact Int64 words).
- Hilbert keys, map colouring, line merge and the rotating-calipers and Welzl bounds hold up.

The failures sit one layer up, where results computed in f32 or by finite approximations decide topology right next to the exact predicate:
- Bowyer-Watson with a finite supertriangle loses hull triangles (P0).
- Buffers are resolved with even-odd parity, so self-overlapping offsets punch holes.
- Make-valid emits hole interiors as polygons.
- Overlay side probes and the `edgeBreaks` collinearity branch use inexact arithmetic.
- The H3 cover lattice is too coarse on tall high-latitude features.
- p-median breaks on unreachable demand.

Most of these are silent: no status bit is raised. The spatial-analysis roadmap marks tranches 5 and 6 "Complete" with fixtures that exercise none of these cases.

## 3. Patterns

1. **Inexact point-in-polygon decides topology next to an exact predicate.**
   - The library has 15 hand-written ray-cast PIP or parity loops.
   - 5 use exact `orient`/`orientSign`: spatial-predicate, spatial-relate ×2, nearest and point-in-polygon-join, plus geometry-validity.
   - 10 use an f32 division crossing: polygon-overlay, ring-assembly :1146 (hole-to-shell assignment), trajectory-zones, cell-cover ×2, polygon-triangulation :633, label-point ×2, dot-density, region-statistics/region-mask, line-density/line-length-per-polygon, and `geospatial/gpu-point-spatial-query.ts:857`.
   - In 3 of these the result is topology: overlay membership, ring hole assignment and zone parity. That is the source of D-05, D-09 and suspicion S-3.
   - Rule: **one shared exact `pointInRingParity`/`segmentCrossesSoS` WGSL helper. f32 crossings are allowed only in sampling code (labels, dots), and they get a comment there.**
2. **Mixed exact/inexact branching.**
   - `edgeBreaks` (D-04) picks its branch on f32 `den`.
   - zone segment tests (D-09) and line-length-per-polygon place breaks with f32 `cross2`.
   - Rule: **every branch that changes a topological classification is decided by exact signs; f32 values only place a point.**
3. **Parity used where winding is meant.** Buffer (D-02) and make-valid (D-03) both rebuild areas from boundaries without the source's fill rule. Rule: **every boundary→area rebuild states and implements its fill rule (nonzero for offsets, source parity for repair), with a fixture where the rule matters.**
4. **Finite sentinels inside sums.** `1e30` as ∞ is harmless in min/max (about 25 sites) but wrong when summed (location-allocation, D-08). Rule: **sentinels may only enter min/max; reachability is a separate count or flag.**
5. **Status that cannot report what went wrong.**
   - `requiredCount == count` in 2 sites (D-10).
   - Uncertain and non-finite input is invisible unless an optional view is wired (D-11).
   - Delaunay hull loss, cover misses and p-median's empty selection raise nothing.
   - Rule: **every silent drop increments a canonical status word.**
6. **Fixtures too small to hit the failure.** The Delaunay spec has 4 points, the cover spec no tall polygon, make-valid no hole, buffer one straight line, p-median no ∞ cost. Rule: **each topology operator gets one randomized or property fixture against its CPU or shapely oracle (counts and invariants), not just hand cases.**

## 4. Roadmap claims checked

- `gpu-analysis-algorithms-roadmap.md` §Review notes: "Exact integer orientation and incircle are already used across the geometry family (12 files), so a new filtered `orient2d` predicate is not needed."
  - The predicate is sound, and 16 files now include it.
  - But overlay membership, ring hole assignment, zone crossings, `edgeBreaks` branching and crossing-point placement use f32 (patterns 1 and 2).
  - "Not needed" is right about the predicate; the implied robustness is not.
- Same doc, item 2: snap-rounding plus face labelling "fixes node drift, the only real robustness gap in the geometry family". Wrong: see D-01, D-02, D-03, D-04, D-05 and D-09. Item 2 does cover D-05 and D-06, so those should be tracked under it.
- `gpu-spatial-analysis-roadmap.md:521`, tranche 5 "Complete … exact overflow requirements": `requiredCount` echoes `count` (D-10), and make-valid of a holed polygon is wrong (D-03).
- `gpu-spatial-analysis-roadmap.md:522`, tranche 6 "Complete … line and signed polygon buffers exercise repair assembly; Delaunay/Voronoi cover duplicate, collinear, cocircular …":
  - Buffers fail on any self-overlap (D-02).
  - Delaunay loses hull triangles on ordinary uniform input (D-01).
  - The spec does not cover these cases.
- `polygon-buffer/gpu-buffer-surface.ts:79-80` ("self-crossing raw offsets … are resolved"): false for winding ≥ 2 regions.
- Node specs in flight (peer edits, not counted as findings):
  - `spatial-join.node.spec.ts` references undefined `GPU_EARTH_MEAN_RADIUS_METERS` and `createRandom`.
  - `gpu-buffer-selection.node.spec.ts` "is planar only" expects a throw that no longer happens.

## 5. Unverified suspicions

- S-1: spatial-relate piece midpoints (spatial-relate-wgsl.ts:134-138, 244-247) are rounded f32 points classified exactly, so a piece within 1 ulp of a near-parallel boundary may be mislocated. To confirm, fuzz near-parallel polygon pairs against shapely `relate`.
- S-2: ring-assembly Chebyshev vertex matching is not transitive. Three points at 0.9·tol can chain in an order-dependent way. To confirm, use a 3-point collinear fixture.
- S-3: ring-assembly hole-to-shell probe (:1122-1148) is the f32 midpoint of the hole's first edge, tested with an f32 ray cast. Holes nearly coincident with the shell may be assigned to the wrong shell or dropped, and dropped holes are not counted.
- S-4: `GPUBufferSurface` negative buffers and mitre limit were not compared with shapely. A negative buffer of a polygon with a thin neck likely shows the D-02 parity problem.
- S-5: label-point falls back to the first vertex (`degenerate=1`) for archipelagos whose parts miss the 3 scanlines and the 16×16 grid. To confirm, test two 0.2-unit squares in a 100-unit extent.
- S-6: cell-set-outline neighbour-across-edge uses a "nearest centre to edge midpoint" rule (:~526), which may be wrong at res 0-1 near the poles or at pentagon edges. To confirm, compare a CPU port with h3-js `cellsToMultiPolygon` over res 0-2.
- S-7: H3 forward assignment near icosahedron face edges in f32. The TSDoc admits grid-neighbour mismatches, but the rate at res 8-10 has not been measured.
- S-8: u32 candidate and pair-count scans could wrap past 4.29e9 without an overflow flag. This is unlikely at current capacities.
- S-9: `abs(x) < 3e38` and `x == x` finite tests in line-merge keys and the minimum-clearance probe may be folded away by Metal; bounds-filter already uses the bit test. To confirm, run on Metal with NaN input.
